The 12 INCHES approach

Good spaces are not just seen. They are experienced.

12 INCHES is an architecture, interior design, construction and fit-out studio based in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh. We believe meaningful spaces begin with listening — understanding the people, purpose and place behind every project.

From early ideas to on-site execution, we bring creativity, practical thinking and attention to detail into one seamless process.
